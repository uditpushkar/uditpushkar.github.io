I have recently started plating the Ghost of Yotie on Playstation. The game is brilliantly designed and engaging to the level that you may spend hours before you realize. The game is not a sequel of Ghost of Tsushima in the strictest sense but yes the gameplay is definitly based on the Tsushima. "<!--more-->"

## Ghost of Tsushima 

I played GoT on year back and it was absolutlely lovely to play. The storyline is deeply rooted in the ancient Japanese culture. The main character Jin, samurai trying to to liberate the island of Tsushima from Mongol conquest. Ghost of Tsushima has made an entire genre of gameplay by itself. The gameplay is designed in a way that you take time and immerse youself going back in time to the islanfd of Tsushima. The mediations, poetry and folk stroies are truely engaging. The photo mode is added bonus in engagement as provides you with opprtunity to click screenshots with highly customizale camera settings. 


## Ghost of Yotie 

Graphics are absolutly amazing for GoY and definitely an upgrade from the GoT. The Yotie storyline is ahead of Tsushima and offers many advaned weaponry such as gunpowder.
