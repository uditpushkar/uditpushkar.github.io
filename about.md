# Hi, I am Udit.

This is my personal website, a space where I write about the things that hold my attention and stay with me. Some posts are travel notes, some are reflections, and some are simply attempts to put a passing thought into words before it disappears.

I currently serve in the Indian Police Service and am posted as Aide-de-Camp to the Hon'ble Governor of Chhattisgarh. Before this assignment, I worked in the Bastar region, including areas affected by Left Wing Extremism. Those years shaped the way I think about public service, duty, and the texture of life away from the obvious centers.
